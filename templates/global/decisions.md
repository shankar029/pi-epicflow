# Global Decisions

> Cross-repo decisions that apply by default when starting new work or
> when there's no per-repo decision on the topic. Lives at
> `~/.pi/global-memory/decisions.md`.
>
> Append-only. Cite by `GD-NNN` (Global Decision) to distinguish from
> per-repo `DEC-NNN`. When a per-repo `decisions.md` has a `DEC-NNN`
> covering the same topic, the per-repo entry wins.
